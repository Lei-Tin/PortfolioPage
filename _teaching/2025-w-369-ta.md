I worked for 60 hours as the teaching assistant for CSC369H1S: Operating Systems, a third-year operating systems course at the University of Toronto.

The instructor for this session was Prof. Kuei (Jack) Sun.

# Duties

- Held weekly office hours to answer student questions
- Graded midterms and the final exam
- Graded assignments and checked for plagiarism
- Proofread course materials and exams

I have also created tutorial materials about buffer overflow attacks, details can be found in the [other post](/teaching/2024-309-369-reform-course).
