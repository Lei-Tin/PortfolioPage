SiniticMTError adds fine-grained annotations of translation error spans, types, and severity to English-to-Mandarin, Cantonese, and Wu Chinese examples, alongside a Mandarin-Hokkien component. The dataset supports translation quality estimation, error detection, and evaluation for low-resource languages.

The paper evaluates open and closed language models on translation error detection and reports native-speaker annotation procedures, pilot studies, and analyses of error patterns.

The full paper appeared at **LREC 2026** and is available in the [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.683/). An earlier version appeared at the [COLM 2025 WMDQS Workshop](https://wmdqs.org/submissions-2025/6.pdf).
